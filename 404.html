How to read NORTHLINE in 30 seconds

  1. Top strip: what the paper bot is doing right now and what would make it buy.
  2. Pulse score (0–100): how strongly the market lines up for a buy. Under 55 means wait, 55–69 means watch, and 70+ with all six checks green means TAKE.
  3. Six checks: tap any chip to see what it means in plain words. The bot only buys when all six are green.
  4. Engine tab: the same score as a living engine. Blue idle means waiting, amber revving means getting close, green redline means TAKE. Pistons misfire for the checks that fail.
  5. Bot tab: the paper account (start capital set in the bot config, now $1,000), its trades and every version that has "died" and rebuilt.
  6. Everything is paper money. Nothing here can place a real trade.
